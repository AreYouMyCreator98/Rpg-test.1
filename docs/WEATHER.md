# Weather and navigation — realm-weather-1

One full day is 24 minutes of active solo play. New or older worlds begin on day 1 at 09:00. The clock lives in `living.climate` (version 1); existing local/cloud world snapshots preserve it without touching character progression. Solo menus pause it. Co-op snapshots carry the host's clock; guests do not advance independently. Leaving a party restores the existing solo snapshot. Time does not jump forward while the game is closed.

Weather is deterministic from game days. Each 14-day window contains one rain-or-storm spell, beginning on a day between 4 and 13 of that window and lasting roughly 4–12 minutes. Forty percent of scheduled wet spells are storms; dry gaps vary with the schedule. Occasional morning fog and afternoon cloud occur independently. The first two days stay fair. A 70-day sample produced five wet days: zero-based 7, 21, 35, 46 and 66. This is a sparse authored schedule, not real-world forecasting or rain every other day.

The existing sun, hemisphere fill, sky shader and fog are modulated continuously, with 12-second weather blends. Night keeps readable moonlit fill; dusk warms sunlight. Overcast changes sky coverage and light, rainfall uses one pooled line draw, and medium-or-higher effects add inexpensive geometric sunlight shafts. These are stylised shafts, not occlusion-aware volumetric ray tracing. Storms have slow illumination glows and generated low thunder; reduced-motion disables glows. Rain audio uses the existing user-gesture-unlocked audio context and environment volume. Dungeons retain their own lighting and exclude outdoor rain/shafts. No new assets, external services or shadow lights are required.

A narrow, camera-oriented N/E/S/W compass opens the actual world map. Its gold marker points to the active tracked quest (or the opening story objective); completed objectives point to their quest giver. Off-screen directions clamp to the compass edge and show a direction chevron. The quest label opens the journal. A day number, clock, day/night phase and weather label sit beneath the compass. Combat controls remain unchanged.

Validation: `tests/weather-browser.cjs` uses actual Chromium WebGL and touch input to check time advancement, snapshot restoration, day/night lighting, sparse rainfall, bearings, story target, storm/sun renders, dungeon isolation, map/journal controls, 320px portrait, landscape and desktop layout, and High-quality shafts. `tests/multiplayer-browser.cjs` checks host clock/weather replication alongside existing room/combat regressions. Physical Android/iPhone performance, Safari rendering and subjective audio quality need real-device testing. Browser screenshots are in `previews/weather/`.
